里程票会受影响吗？

Will Award Tickets Be Affected?

好消息：里程票本身不收燃油附加费！但以下几点需要注意：

Good news: award tickets don't have fuel surcharges! But watch for these:

✅ 里程票 不含燃油费 ✅ Award Tickets no fuel surcharge ⚠️ 航司可能 上调里程需求 ⚠️ Airlines may raise mileage costs ⚠️ 座位可能 减少 ⚠️ Availability may shrink

为什么里程票更划算？现金票要付燃油附加费(约$50-150)，而里程票只付税费+少量YQ。现在航司现金票涨价，里程票的相对价值反而提升了！

Why award tickets are better: Cash tickets include fuel surcharges ($50-150). Award tickets only pay taxes + minimal YQ. With cash fares rising, award ticket value improves!

应对策略：3招帮你省钱

Strategy: 3 Ways to Save

  • 1
    尽快锁定里程票 Lock in award tickets now

    航司通常提前330天放票。现在正是锁定下半年出行里程票的好时机，尤其是亚洲-欧洲航线。Amex MR、Chase UR、Citi TYP 都能转航司。

    Airlines release seats ~330 days out. Lock in award tickets for late 2026 now, especially Asia-Europe routes. Amex MR, Chase UR, Citi TYP all transfer to airlines.

  • 2
    避开中东航司，选寰宇/星空 Avoid Middle East carriers, use Oneworld/Star Alliance

    卡塔尔、阿联酋EK等中东航司的燃油附加费本来就高，现在更贵。改走国泰(寰宇)、新加坡航空(星空)更稳定。

    Qatar and Emirates already have high surcharges — now even higher. Cathay (Oneworld) and Singapore Airlines (Star Alliance) are more stable.

  • 3
    考虑开口程/停留，最大化里程价值 Use open-jaws/stops to maximize mile value

    比如换北京-伦敦开口程，中间巴黎停留，12.5万亚万就能搞定(需绕过大陆出发限制)。现金票涨了，里程票相当于变相升值。

    e.g., Beijing-London open-jaw with Paris stop, ~125k Asia Miles. With cash fares up, your miles are worth more.

接下来会怎样？

What's Next?

分析师警告：如果冲突持续，航煤供应可能进一步紧张。部分对冲了燃油成本的航司(如英航IAG、汉莎)短期内不会大幅涨价，但中小航司可能继续加价。

Analysts warn: if the conflict continues, jet fuel supply could tighten further. Airlines with fuel hedges (BA/IAG, Lufthansa) won't raise prices dramatically short-term, but smaller carriers may continue hiking.

建议：如果你有下半年出行计划，现在用里程锁定。特别是中美/中欧商务舱，现金票涨起来肉疼，里程票且用且珍惜。

Tip: If you have travel plans for H2 2026, lock in award tickets now. Especially for China-US/Europe business class — cash fares hurt, award tickets are precious.
